## Overview

Liam worked as an engineer within Fender’s product and design direction, translating a polished visual concept into resilient production interaction. This case study does not claim authorship of Fender’s visual design.

## Production interaction

The React-based gallery work included responsive layouts, sticky desktop behavior, masonry and carousel presentation, variant-driven product media, 360-degree media and modal interaction, and loading and skeleton states.

## Reusable frontend architecture

The experience needed reusable architecture, performance consideration, and careful handling of Safari and other browser edge cases. The work supported multi-brand ecommerce use across Fender, Jackson, and PreSonus where appropriate.
