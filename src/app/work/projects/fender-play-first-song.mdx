## Overview

Liam worked with Fender’s product designers to translate strong static Figma screens into a production onboarding experience. The design team owned the original product and visual direction.

## From states to interaction

The screens described key states, but not every moment of motion and interaction between them. Liam’s engineering work included translating that intent into production and making interaction and motion decisions where a static handoff could not fully specify behavior.

## Implementation

The experience used Lottie and the Web Animations API for complex onboarding transitions. The work combined production implementation, close collaboration with product and design, and detailed decisions about the moments between static screens.
