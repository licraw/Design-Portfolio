## Existing site

Liam led the redesign and rebuild of PaintPDX’s website. The existing experience was an older WordPress site.

## What needed to stay

The project did not begin by discarding the old site’s personality. Liam identified the parts the owner valued and used those as constraints for the new work.

## Redesign

The redesign preserved that character while creating a clearer, more modern interface for the Portland painting company.

## Responsive implementation

Liam translated the redesign into a responsive frontend that works across screen sizes.

## Result

The rebuilt site is live at [paintpdx.com](https://paintpdx.com). No conversion or business metrics are claimed here.
