## What it is

Portland Painting & Restoration is a painting and restoration contractor serving Portland and Vancouver. Their site was an aging WordPress build.

Liam led the redesign and the rebuild — visual design, the design file, and the front-end implementation. The site is live at [paintpdx.com](https://paintpdx.com).

## What had to stay

A small contractor's site is not a blank canvas. The logo, the green, the phone number in the top bar, and the plain-spoken tone were the parts the owner valued and customers recognized, so they were treated as fixed inputs rather than things to improve.

What changed was everything around them: hierarchy, type scale, spacing, image treatment, and the way services are presented.

## Result

The site is live and responsive, and the business's identity survived the rebuild. No conversion, traffic or revenue claims are made here.

## Notes on media

The pre-redesign site is not shown. The archived copies of the old WordPress build no longer render with their stylesheets, and a reconstruction would not be an honest before state.
