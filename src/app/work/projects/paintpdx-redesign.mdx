## What it is

Portland Painting & Restoration is a painting and restoration contractor serving Portland and Vancouver. Their site was an aging WordPress build.

Liam led the redesign and the rebuild — visual design, the design file, and the front-end implementation. The site is live at [paintpdx.com](https://paintpdx.com).

## The problem

The business needed a modern, responsive site that made its painting, carpentry, and restoration services easier to understand without discarding the identity its owner and customers already recognized. The customer path needed to answer three things quickly: what the company does, whether it serves an interior or exterior need, and how to request an estimate.

## What had to stay

A small contractor's site is not a blank canvas. The logo, the green, the phone number in the top bar, and the plain-spoken tone were the parts the owner valued and customers recognized, so they were treated as fixed inputs rather than things to improve.

What changed was everything around them: hierarchy, type scale, spacing, image treatment, and the way services are presented.

## Visual direction and system

The existing logo and green remained the brand anchors. A larger type scale, restrained image treatment, and more deliberate spacing shifted the visual direction without turning the company into something unfamiliar. The hierarchy leads with the service promise, follows with service navigation and credibility, and keeps the estimate action available in both desktop and collapsed navigation.

## Service navigation in the built site

Painting, carpentry, and restoration form the primary service structure. Within each card, an interior/exterior control lets customers change context without doubling the number of cards or forcing a longer page. That interaction and its content hierarchy carry from the Figma design into the shipped site.

## Responsive

The layout was designed as one responsive system rather than a desktop page with content removed for mobile. Navigation collapses into a full-screen panel, the estimate call to action remains prominent, content stacks in reading order, and service cards retain the interior/exterior interaction at narrow widths.

## Result

The Figma design, style guide, and responsive interaction decisions were carried into the production build Liam implemented. The site is live and responsive, and the business's identity survived the rebuild. No conversion, traffic or revenue claims are made here.

The pre-redesign site is not shown. The archived copies of the old WordPress build no longer render with their stylesheets, and a reconstruction would not be an honest before state.
