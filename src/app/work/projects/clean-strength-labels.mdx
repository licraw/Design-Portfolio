## What it is

An adaptable visual identity and label system for Clean Strength products, designed to hold together across creatine, 5-HTP, and protein products while adapting to different container formats.

## A hierarchy that adapts

The work is typographic. A physical label has a fixed area, necessary product information, and a curved surface it has to stay legible on. The system establishes a repeatable reading order: Clean Strength brand, product name, variant, required information, weight or quantity, and secondary claims. Scale, weight, color, and spacing keep those levels distinct without crowding the front face.

## Consistency across products

The blue, black, and white palette, strong product-name typography, and repeated information zones make the range recognizable as one brand. Product names and variants change emphasis where each format needs it, while the shared hierarchy keeps creatine, 5-HTP, and protein connected rather than treating every label as a separate composition.
