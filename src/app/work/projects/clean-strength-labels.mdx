## What it is

A collection of product labels for Clean Strength, designed by Liam in Canva.

The work is typographic. A label has a fixed area, a required set of legally and practically necessary information, and a curved surface it has to stay legible on. Hierarchy has to survive all three: product name first, variant second, everything else where it belongs without crowding the front face.
