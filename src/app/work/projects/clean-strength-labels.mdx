## Packaging to storefront

Clean Strength is my owned and operated supplement brand and Shopify store. I established its visual identity through the product labels and current storefront, then extended that identity into a reusable ecommerce design system and responsive PDP redesign.

## Brand foundation

The labels established the system first: a blue, black, and white palette, strong product-name typography, and repeatable information zones that distinguish products without breaking the family resemblance. The current Shopify theme carries that same identity through its color, type, product imagery, and Theme Editor customizations.

The existing Supliful-based theme is a functional foundation, but many product-specific templates repeat the same PDP structure. ## A reusable ecommerce system

The digital system translates the packaging’s direct typography and high-contrast palette into semantic color and type tokens, responsive spacing, interaction states, and components. Buttons, purchase options, quantity controls, galleries, accordions, product cards, and the mobile sticky action share one source of truth.

## Complete Multivitamin PDP

The redesigned PDP gives the product imagery and thumbnails a clear stage, then organizes name, verified attributes, price, pack choice, purchase option, quantity, and add-to-cart behavior into one legible decision path. Benefits and detailed product information follow in scannable sections, with accordions providing progressive disclosure rather than an uninterrupted description.

## One hierarchy across breakpoints

Desktop pairs the gallery and purchase panel; mobile sequences them for fast scanning, shortens the thumbnail treatment, and keeps the active purchase available once the primary controls leave the viewport. The information order stays consistent even as the layout changes.

## Designed for Shopify

The implementation model is a canonical PDP assembled from reusable sections and blocks. Shared layout and behavior remain consistent; product benefits, ingredients, imagery, FAQs, and other legitimate differences enter through product-specific content slots and structured metafields.

That gives the Theme Editor useful flexibility without returning to duplicated templates. It is a practical bridge between polished ecommerce UI and the way a Shopify storefront is actually maintained.
